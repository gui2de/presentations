Reading the comparison. Berkshire Hathaway's 19.7% benchmark is the compounded annual increase in its per-share market value from 1965 through 2025, as reported in its 2025 annual report. Historical market CAGR and hypothetical cash-flow IRR are not identical investment experiences. Earnings are assumed to be causal *incremental* earnings attributable to the PhD, not total labour income. The opportunity-cost slider captures foregone income as a lump-sum year-0 cost, rather than modelling the years of study separately. All inputs and figures are nominal.
